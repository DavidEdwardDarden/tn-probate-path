The rule changed in 2023

Public Chapter 297, effective April 28, 2023, replaced the small estate affidavit with a sworn petition for Limited Letters, rewrote the bond rules, and barred creditor notice and claims from the small estate track. Guides still using the old affidavit wording are out of date, and some county fee schedules still print the old name over the new form.

Looking for the small estate affidavit?

Tennessee repealed it in 2023. A sworn petition for Limited Letters took its place, and a great many pages still describe the old process. If that is what brought you here, start there.

Which office takes probate where you are

Most Tennessee counties file probate with the Clerk and Master. Six confirmed do not, one of them Nashville, and five more are unsettled. There is no published list of this anywhere, so one was built from the statutes and every county's private acts.

This is organization, not legal advice. It points you at the right filing so you are not reading the wrong form at the clerk's window. Tennessee probate is handled by the Clerk and Master of the Chancery Court in most counties, and a few counties have their own probate court with local rules.

The 2023 rewrite replaced an affidavit that was already court-filed, under the Small Estate Affidavit Limited Letter of Authority Act. It made it a petition, prescribed a form for the letters, rewrote bond, and barred creditor notice and claims from the small estate track. TennCare estate recovery applies to long-term care received at age 55 or older; the estate representative files a Request for Release with TennCare.

Court costs, local forms, and whether a county entertains a muniment of title all vary. The Clerk and Master in the county where the person lived is the authority on all three, and the statute directs that office to help you with the small estate petition.
